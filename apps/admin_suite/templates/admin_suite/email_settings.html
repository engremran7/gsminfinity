Gmail SMTP (App Password)

Configure Gmail delivery using an app password. From will default to the Gmail username when left blank.

Deliverability tips

  • Add SPF for your sending domain: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all.
  • Ensure DKIM is enabled in your Gmail account (Admin console > Apps > Gmail > Authenticate email).
  • Set a DMARC record (e.g., v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:postmaster@yourdomain.com).
  • Use a dedicated From name/domain and keep templates consistent to avoid spam classification.
